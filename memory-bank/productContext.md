# Product Context - YTDx

## Purpose & Why This Project Exists
YTDx was designed to provide users with a clean, responsive, and robust desktop YouTube downloader without bloated third-party adware or complicated command-line requirements.

## Key Problems Solved
- **Ease of Use**: Simple UI allowing one-click download of video/audio from YouTube URLs.
- **Format Flexibility**: Download video in best quality or specific resolution; extract audio as MP3 with embedded album art and metadata.
- **Reliability**: Uses `pytubefix` and FFmpeg to handle stream merging (combining video-only and audio-only YouTube adaptive streams).
- **Localization**: Native multi-language support (TR/EN) for broader accessibility.
